Hotel Booking Data Analysis | MySQL

Analyzed 5,000 hotel booking records using MySQL to evaluate booking patterns, cancellation behavior, revenue performance, market segments, hotel performance, country-level trends, and monthly revenue.

Key SQL techniques:
SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, ORDER BY, aggregate functions, CASE WHEN, CTEs, date functions, and window functions including RANK().

Key findings:
• Total booking value: 200.12M
• Realized revenue: 52.41M
• Revenue realization rate: 26.19%
• City Hotel contributed 66.54% of realized revenue.
• Online bookings contributed 48.62% of realized revenue.
